About Marbella

Marbella is the jewel of the Costa del Sol — a glamorous resort city synonymous with luxury living, celebrity sightings, and the celebrated Golden Mile (Milla de Oro) where iconic five-star hotels line the beach. The medieval old town (Casco Antiguo) offers a beautiful historic contrast, with orange-tree-lined squares and boutique restaurants. Puerto Banús — Europe's most famous luxury marina — sits 6 km to the west.
