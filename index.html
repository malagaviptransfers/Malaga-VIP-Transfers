Malaga Airport Code AGP

Malaga airport code AGP — officially Aeropuerto de Málaga-Costa del Sol (Pablo Ruiz Picasso Airport). Located 8 km south-west of Malaga city centre, AGP is Spain's fourth-busiest airport, handling approximately 22 million passengers annually. All our transfers use Terminal 3.

Malaga Airport to Marbella

Malaga Airport to Marbella by private transfer takes approximately 45 minutes (60 km). The malaga airport marbella route is our most popular. By Malaga Airport to Marbella bus, the journey requires a change and takes over an hour. Our fixed-price private car is the fastest and most comfortable option.

Malaga Airport to Benalmadena

Malaga airport Benalmadena is a 20-minute drive (17 km). Whether you're heading to Benalmadena Pueblo, El Higueron, or the coast, a transfer from Malaga Airport to Benalmadena by private car is direct and takes no more than 25 minutes. From €55 per vehicle — Benalmadena to Malaga Airport return also available.

Malaga airport to Malaga centre is just 8 km — around 15–20 minutes by private car. Our taxi Malaga airport to city centre service runs 24/7. The Malaga airport to Malaga city centre transfer starts from €66 per vehicle with a fixed price, free meet-and-greet and flight monitoring included.

A shuttle transfer Malaga Airport (shared bus) stops at multiple hotels and can add hours to your journey. Airport transfers Malaga Airport with us are always private — your own vehicle, your own schedule, zero waiting for other passengers. Airport transfers Spain Malaga — at their finest.
