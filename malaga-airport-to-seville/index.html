About Seville

Seville is Andalusia's capital and one of Europe's most passionate and beautiful cities — home to the world's third-largest cathedral, the magnificent Alcázar Royal Palace (a UNESCO site), the labyrinthine Santa Cruz quarter, and the deeply atmospheric Semana Santa (Holy Week) and Feria de Abril festivals. A private transfer from Malaga Airport to Seville avoids the need for rail or flight connections.
