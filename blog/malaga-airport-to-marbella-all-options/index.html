How to Get from Malaga Airport to Marbella — All Your Options

Malaga VIP Transfers • Airport Guide • 7 min read

Arriving at Malaga Airport (AGP) and heading to the glamorous resort town of Marbella? The Malaga Airport to Marbella route is one of the most-travelled transfers on the Costa del Sol — and there are four main ways to make the journey. In this guide, we compare every option on price, travel time, and convenience so you can decide what works best for your trip.

Distance from Malaga Airport to Marbella

Malaga Airport to Marbella is approximately 60 kilometres by road. The most direct route takes the AP-7 motorway (with a small toll), which under normal conditions delivers a drive time of 45 to 55 minutes. Taking the A-7 coastal road avoids the toll but adds around 10–15 minutes and passes through several towns.

If you are travelling to specific areas of Marbella — such as Puerto Banús, the Golden Mile, or Nueva Andalucía — the distance and time can vary slightly. Puerto Banús, for example, is around 55 km from the airport and can be reached in as little as 40 minutes on a quiet morning.

Option 1 — Private Transfer from Malaga Airport to Marbella

A private airport transfer is the option chosen by most visitors staying in Marbella's luxury hotels and villas. With Malaga VIP Transfers, your professional chauffeur meets you inside the arrivals hall holding a name board, loads your luggage, and drives you directly to your hotel or villa door in an exclusively Mercedes-Benz vehicle.

  • Price: From €110 per vehicle (fixed, no meter, no hidden charges)
  • Travel time: 45–55 minutes direct
  • Vehicles: Mercedes E-Class, S-Class (up to 3 passengers), V-Class (up to 7 passengers), Minibus (up to 19), Luxury Coach (up to 30)
  • Flight tracking: Your driver monitors your flight in real time — no extra charge for delays
  • Waiting time: 60 minutes free wait after landing included

Why choose a private transfer? For a family of four sharing one vehicle, a private Malaga Airport to Marbella transfer often costs less per person than four individual bus tickets once you factor in luggage, time, and getting to your actual hotel.

Option 2 — Standard Taxi from Malaga Airport to Marbella

The standard taxi rank at Malaga Airport is located immediately outside the arrivals hall in Terminal 3. Taxis from Malaga Airport to Marbella use a meter, plus a series of supplements — these include airport pick-up surcharges (typically €5.45), night and weekend rates, and luggage charges. The final bill for a taxi from Malaga Airport to Marbella typically ranges from €90 to €130, depending on the exact destination and time of day.

During peak summer months (July–August), taxi queues at the airport can be significant — waits of 30–45 minutes are common during busy arrival waves. Unlike a pre-booked private transfer, a taxi offers no price certainty before you get in.

Option 3 — Bus from Malaga Airport to Marbella

The M-221 bus service (operated by Portillo/Avanza) runs from Malaga Airport to Marbella bus station. The Malaga Airport to Marbella bus is the cheapest option, costing approximately €6–8 per person.

The downsides: the bus stops at multiple towns along the Costa del Sol (Torremolinos, Benalmádena, Fuengirola, and others), making the journey considerably longer than a direct transfer. It also deposits you at the Marbella bus station, not your hotel — meaning onward travel is required with luggage. Luggage space is limited during peak season.

Option 4 — Train + Taxi Combination

Marbella has no train station, so there is no direct train from Malaga Airport to Marbella. The closest you can get by train is Fuengirola, which is around 20 km east of Marbella. The Cercanías C1 line runs from the airport station to Fuengirola in approximately 40 minutes (€3.05 fare), from where you would need an onward taxi — costing approximately €25–35 to Marbella town centre, bringing total cost to around €30–40 per person and total travel time to over 90 minutes.

When to Book Your Malaga Airport to Marbella Transfer

Marbella is one of Europe's most in-demand summer destinations. July and August see the Malaga Airport at maximum capacity, with hundreds of transfers departing simultaneously. Booking your Malaga Airport to Marbella private transfer at least 24 hours in advance is recommended — ideally at the time of booking your flights or accommodation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Malaga Airport to Marbella?

Malaga Airport to Marbella is approximately 60 km by road. In normal traffic, this is a 45–55 minute drive via the AP-7 motorway.

What is the best way to get from Malaga Airport to Marbella?

For most travellers, a private airport transfer from Malaga to Marbella offers the best combination of comfort, speed, and certainty of price. For budget travellers travelling light, the M-221 bus is a practical but slower option.

Is there Uber in Malaga?

Uber currently operates with limited availability in the Malaga area and is not a reliable option for airport transfers from Malaga to Marbella. Bolt operates more consistently, though surge pricing can make it comparable in cost to a pre-booked private transfer.

How much does the bus cost from Malaga Airport to Marbella?

The M-221 bus costs approximately €6–8 per person from Malaga Airport to Marbella bus station, with a journey time of around 90–120 minutes.
