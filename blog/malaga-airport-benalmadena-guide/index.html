Malaga Airport to Benalmadena — Complete Transfer Guide

Malaga VIP Transfers•Route Guide•7 min read

Benalmadena is one of the most popular arrivals destinations from Malaga Airport. Whether you're heading to Benalmadena Costa, Benalmadena Pueblo, or El Higueron, this guide covers every transport option, current prices, and exactly how long your journey will take.

How far is Malaga Airport from Benalmadena?

Malaga Airport to Benalmadena is approximately 17 km by road, via the A-7 coastal motorway. By car, the journey takes 20–25 minutes in normal traffic conditions. During summer peak hours (July–August), allow 30–40 minutes. The distance makes Malaga airport Benalmadena one of the shorter transfers on the Costa del Sol — less than half an hour from arrivals to your hotel.

Malaga Airport to Benalmadena El Higueron

El Higueron (Higuerón Resort) is a specific area of upper Benalmadena, set on the hillside above the motorway. Malaga airport to Benalmadena El Higueron by private transfer takes approximately 25 minutes. It is not well served by public transport — a taxi or private transfer is by far the most practical option. From €55 per vehicle, fixed.

Malaga Airport to Benalmadena Pueblo

Benalmadena Pueblo — the traditional white village set high above the coast — is approximately 20 km from the airport and takes around 25–30 minutes by car. It is not directly served by bus from the airport. A private transfer from Malaga Airport to Benalmadena Pueblo is included in our standard Benalmadena pricing — from €55 per vehicle.
