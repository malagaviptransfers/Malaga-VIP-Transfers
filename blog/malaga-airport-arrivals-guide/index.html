Malaga Airport Arrivals: What to Expect as a First-Time Visitor

Malaga VIP Transfers•Airport Guide•7 min read

If you are flying into Malaga Airport (AGP) for the first time, this guide walks you through exactly what to expect from the moment your plane touches down to the moment you meet your driver. Malaga Airport — officially Pablo Ruiz Picasso Airport — is a modern, well-organised facility and one of the busiest in Spain. Arriving here is straightforward, but knowing the layout in advance makes everything faster.

Step-by-Step: Arriving at Malaga Airport

Step 1 — Landing & Taxiing

After landing at Malaga Airport, your plane will taxi to a gate at Terminal 3 (the main terminal). Most flights use airbridge-connected stands; some lower-cost carriers use remote stands with bus transfers to the terminal. The terminal is large but well-signposted in English.

Step 2 — Passport Control

EU/Schengen passport holders pass through the EU lane, which typically moves quickly. Since Brexit, British passport holders must queue in the Non-EU lane and may have their passports stamped; allow 20–40 minutes during peak season. Non-EU visitors (USA, Canada, Australia, etc.) also use the Non-EU lane. The Non-EU lanes can be slow in summer during simultaneous wide-body arrivals — arrival time on your transfer booking should account for this.

Malaga Airport is introducing e-gates for more nationalities — check current eligibility when travelling.

Step 3 — Baggage Claim

Baggage carousels are on the ground floor of Terminal 3, clearly signposted from passport control. Your carousel number is displayed on the digital boards near the top of the escalators. Baggage delivery times at Malaga Airport vary — in summer, allow 20–35 minutes from disembarkation for your bags to appear, particularly for large aircraft.

Step 4 — Customs

After collecting luggage, pass through customs — Green channel (nothing to declare) or Red channel. EU imports: no personal limits on goods purchased within the EU. Non-EU imports: standard limits apply (1L spirits, 200 cigarettes, goods up to €300 in value from outside the EU).

Step 5 — Arrivals Hall & Meeting Your Driver

Exiting customs, you enter the Malaga Airport arrivals hall — a large, well-lit area with cafés, car rental desks, ATMs, and information points. Private transfer drivers with name boards wait in the designated meeting area immediately ahead and to the right as you exit customs. With Malaga VIP Transfers, your driver will hold a board with your name and will have been monitoring your flight in real time.

Important: If your flight is delayed, do not worry about your driver. Malaga VIP Transfers monitors all flights in real time and adjusts pick-up times automatically. Your driver will be there when you land, regardless of delay.

What's in the Arrivals Hall?

  • ATMs: Multiple cashpoints in arrivals — though card payments are universally accepted across the Costa del Sol
  • Currency Exchange: Available in arrivals but rates are typically poor — use an ATM or exchange before travelling
  • Car Rental: All major companies (Hertz, Europcar, Sixt, Avis, Enterprise) have desks in or adjacent to arrivals
  • Tourist Information: Malaga Tourism office in arrivals with maps, hotel guides, and Costa del Sol information
  • Cafés & Restaurants: Several options in the arrivals area for coffee and snacks post-flight
  • Pharmacy: Open in arrivals during operating hours
