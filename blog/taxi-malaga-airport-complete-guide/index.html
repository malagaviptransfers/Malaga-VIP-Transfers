Taxi Malaga Airport — Complete Guide to Prices, Options & Private Transfers

Malaga VIP Transfers•Taxi Guide•9 min read

If you've just landed at Malaga Airport (AGP) and are wondering whether to take a taxi or pre-book a private transfer — this guide gives you everything you need. We cover malaga airport taxi prices to every major Costa del Sol destination, typical wait times, where the taxi rank is, and why increasing numbers of travellers are choosing a private transfer Malaga Airport instead of a metered cab.

Where is the Malaga taxi rank?

The taxi Malaga Airport rank is located immediately outside the arrivals exit of Terminal 3. Follow signs for "Taxis" — it is a short walk from the baggage reclaim area. Taxis are white and marked with the official Malaga municipality badge. Malaga airport taxi drivers are licensed and metered, and the rank operates 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Key fact: Standard malaga taxi fares are metered (taxímetro). There is a fixed supplement for departures from Malaga Airport — typically €3–5 — added on top of the metered fare. Night-time and weekend surcharges also apply.

Are taxis expensive in Malaga?

Are taxis expensive in Malaga? For short trips within the city, no — a metered malaga taxi is competitively priced by European standards. For airport transfers to the Costa del Sol, the meter can run high, especially on the motorway (AP-7) with its toll charges added to the fare. Longer journeys like malaga airport to nerja taxi or taxi malaga airport to marbella often cost more by meter than a pre-booked fixed-price private transfer.

Malaga taxi to airport — departures

If you're taking a malaga taxi to airport for your departure, you can hail a taxi from any major taxi rank in the city, or call a local radio taxi company. However, there is no guarantee of availability at peak times, and metered fares can vary significantly. Booking a private malaga airport transfer in advance removes all uncertainty — your driver is confirmed, flight departure is monitored, and the price is fixed.
