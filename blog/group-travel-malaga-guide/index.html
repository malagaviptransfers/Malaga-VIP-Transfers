Travelling to Malaga in a Group? Here Is What You Need to Know

Malaga VIP Transfers•Airport Guide•7 min read

Travelling to Malaga or the Costa del Sol as a group introduces a set of logistics that solo or couple travel avoids. Malaga Airport (AGP) is one of the busiest in Spain during summer, and coordinating a group arrival — multiple passengers, multiple bags, potentially multiple flight arrivals — requires planning. This guide covers everything a group organiser needs to know.

Why Group Travel Needs Different Planning

Groups face specific challenges at airports: multiple taxis required (and the cost adds up), coordinating passengers across different vehicles, waiting time for large numbers through passport control and baggage claim. A Mercedes V-Class group transfer from Malaga Airport solves most of these problems — up to 7 passengers and their luggage in one vehicle, all arriving together.

Pricing for Group Transfers at Malaga Airport

Group transfers from Malaga Airport are priced per vehicle, not per person — meaning the cost efficiency significantly improves with group size. A group transfer in a Mercedes V-Class from Malaga Airport to Marbella for 7 people typically costs around €143 for the whole vehicle — considerably less than four or five separate taxis.

Tips for Coordinating Group Arrivals

1. Book Early

During peak season (July–August), Malaga Airport group transfers should be booked at least 2–4 weeks in advance. Premium vehicles, particularly the V-Class for groups, are in high demand. Booking early also locks in prices before seasonal adjustments.

2. Share One Booking Reference

When travelling on multiple flights, coordinate all group members under one booking reference and provide all flight numbers to your transfer company. Malaga VIP Transfers monitors all flights in real time and coordinates the driver to be present for the latest arrival.

3. Designate One Person for Baggage

In the baggage claim area, designate one person to collect bags while others proceed to find the driver in arrivals. This speeds up the process considerably for groups.

4. Plan for Passport Control Time

Non-EU passport holders (UK, USA, Canada, Australia, etc.) face Non-EU queues at Malaga Airport which can be lengthy in peak summer. Add 30–45 minutes to your estimated arrival time from landing to arrivals hall during July–August.
