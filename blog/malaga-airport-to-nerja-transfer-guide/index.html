Malaga Airport to Nerja: Transfer Guide & What to Expect

Malaga VIP Transfers • Airport Guide • 6 min read

Nerja is one of the most beautiful towns on the Costa del Sol — a whitewashed gem perched above dramatic sea cliffs with the famous Balcón de Europa at its heart. If you are flying into Malaga Airport (AGP) and heading east to Nerja, this guide covers everything you need to know about the Malaga Airport to Nerja journey: distances, transport options, prices, and practical tips.

How Far is Malaga Airport to Nerja?

Malaga Airport to Nerja is approximately 55 kilometres by road. The primary route is via the N-340 / A-7 coastal road heading east from Malaga. Depending on traffic — which can be heavy in summer — the drive takes between 45 and 65 minutes. There is no motorway toll on this route.

Nerja is located beyond the busy resort strip of Torremolinos, Benalmádena, and Fuengirola, and sits just before the dramatic cliff coastline that marks the eastern Costa del Sol. This position east of the main resort zone means a transfer from Malaga Airport to Nerja travels against the main flow of summer traffic, often making the journey smoother than routes heading west towards Marbella.

Option 1 — Private Transfer from Malaga Airport to Nerja

A private airport transfer to Nerja is by far the most comfortable and convenient choice. Your professional chauffeur greets you in the arrivals hall at Malaga Airport with a name board, loads your luggage into a premium Mercedes-Benz vehicle, and drives you directly to your hotel, apartment, or villa in Nerja — door to door.

  • Price: From €121 per vehicle (fixed, agreed in advance)
  • Travel time: 50–65 minutes direct
  • Flight tracking: Included — your driver knows if your flight is delayed
  • Waiting time: 60 minutes free after landing
  • Vehicles available: Mercedes E-Class (up to 3 passengers + luggage), Mercedes V-Class (up to 7 passengers)

Travel Tip: Nerja is reached via a winding coastal road in its final section. For travellers prone to motion sickness, requesting a slightly slower pace through these final kilometres is perfectly normal — our drivers are happy to accommodate.

Option 2 — Bus from Malaga to Nerja

There is no direct bus service from Malaga Airport to Nerja. To travel by bus, you must first reach Malaga city bus station (Estación de Autobuses de Málaga), either by taxi or train from the airport, then connect with an Alsa bus to Nerja. Services run regularly from Malaga bus station, with a journey time of approximately 1 hour 30 minutes to Nerja bus stop. Total journey time from airport to Nerja by bus is typically 2 to 2.5 hours.

Alsa buses to Nerja cost approximately €4–6 per person from Malaga bus station. While economical, this option involves multiple changes and significant waiting time — not ideal for arrivals with luggage, families, or late-night flights.

Option 3 — Standard Taxi from Malaga Airport to Nerja

Airport taxis to Nerja are available from the taxi rank outside Terminal 3 arrivals. A metered taxi from Malaga Airport to Nerja typically costs €70–100, depending on traffic, time of day, and supplements (airport surcharge, night rate, and luggage charges all apply). Unlike a pre-booked transfer, there is no guaranteed fixed price and no driver waiting for you specifically.

Option 4 — Train (Cercanías) + Taxi

The Cercanías C1 train runs from Malaga Airport station to Malaga Centro-Alameda in approximately 12 minutes. However, there is no train from Malaga to Nerja — the train line does not extend east of Malaga city. From Malaga city centre, you would need to take a bus (1.5 hr) or taxi (40–50 min, approximately €50–60) to complete the journey to Nerja. This multi-mode approach adds considerable time and complexity.

What to Expect in Nerja

Nerja rewards visitors with the iconic Balcón de Europa — a clifftop promenade with sweeping Atlantic views — and a collection of hidden cove beaches including Burriana, Calahonda, and Maro. The town is compact, authentic, and largely untouched by the high-rise development that characterises some other Costa del Sol resorts.

  • Cuevas de Nerja: Stunning prehistoric caves with a concert hall inside — a must-visit
  • Burriana Beach: Nerja's largest and most lively beach, just a short walk from the centre
  • Balcón de Europa: The famous clifftop viewpoint — ideal at sunset
  • Maro Cascades: Stunning freshwater waterfalls into the sea, accessible by kayak
