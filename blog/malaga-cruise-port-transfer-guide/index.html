Arriving at Malaga Cruise Port: Best Transfer Options & Day Trips

Malaga VIP Transfers•Travel Tips•7 min read

Malaga is one of the most popular Mediterranean cruise stops, and with good reason — within a short taxi or transfer ride of the port sits one of Spain's most atmospheric old towns, world-class museums, excellent beaches, and some of the best day-trip destinations in southern Europe. If your ship is stopping at Malaga cruise port, this guide maximises your time ashore.

About Malaga Cruise Port

Malaga cruise terminal is located directly in the city's main port (Puerto de Málaga), approximately 1.5 km from the historic city centre. The port handles several cruise lines and can accommodate large vessels including the world's largest cruise ships. The cruise passenger terminal is modern, well-equipped, and has tourist information, taxis, and city information available immediately on disembarkation.

Getting from Malaga Cruise Port to the City Centre

The city centre is walkable from the cruise terminal — approximately 15–20 minutes on foot along the pleasant harbour front promenade. Alternatively, a short taxi ride costs around €6–10 and takes 5–8 minutes. A private transfer from Malaga port is available for groups or those wanting a dedicated vehicle for the day.

Things to Do from Malaga Port

Within Walking Distance (0–20 minutes)

  • Malaga Old Town & Atarazanas Market: The 14th-century market hall and surrounding pedestrian streets are 15 minutes walk from the port
  • Málaga Cathedral (La Manquita): One of Spain's finest Renaissance cathedrals, 15 minutes walk
  • Picasso Museum: 20 minutes walk from the port — allow 1–2 hours inside
  • La Malagueta Beach: 25 minutes walk along the promenade — excellent for a swim and espetos

Short Taxi/Transfer Ride (10–20 minutes)

  • La Alcazaba: The magnificent Moorish fortress above the city — 10–15 min by taxi
  • Gibralfaro Castle: Above the Alcazaba, connected by path — panoramic views over the city and port
  • El Palo Beach: A more local, traditional fishing neighbourhood east of the city with excellent informal restaurants

Returning to the Cruise Port

Allow a generous buffer when planning your return to the port — 45–60 minutes before scheduled all-aboard time. Factor in traffic, particularly during summer afternoons. With a pre-booked private transfer from Malaga, your driver knows your deadline and will manage the return journey accordingly.
