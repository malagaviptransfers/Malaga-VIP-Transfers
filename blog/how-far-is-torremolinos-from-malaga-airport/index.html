How far is Torremolinos from Malaga Airport?

Malaga VIP Transfers • Travel Guides • 4 min read

If you are planning a holiday on the Costa del Sol, you might be wondering exactly how far is Torremolinos from Malaga Airport. The good news is that Torremolinos is the closest major resort town to the airport, making it incredibly convenient for quick getaways and family holidays.

Distance and Transfer Times

The distance from Malaga Airport (AGP) to Torremolinos is approximately 8 to 10 kilometers, depending on exactly where in Torremolinos your hotel or accommodation is located. The journey is extremely short and straightforward.

Mode of Transport Estimated Time Convenience Level
Private Transfer (Malaga VIP) 12 - 15 minutes Excellent (Door-to-door, no waiting)
Local Train (Cercanías) 10 minutes + walking Good (Requires walking to hotel with luggage)
Standard Taxi 15 - 20 minutes Moderate (Queue times during peak season)

Exploring Torremolinos

Once you've made the short journey, Torremolinos has a wealth of attractions to offer. From the bustling Calle San Miguel for shopping and tapas, to the beautiful stretches of sand at Playamar and La Carihuela.

  • La Carihuela: The beautiful traditional coastal district, famous for its golden sand beaches and lively promenade.
  • Aqualand Torremolinos: The largest water park on the Costa del Sol, perfect for families.
  • Parque de la Batería: A sprawling green park with a boating lake and viewing tower.
