Malaga Airport Departures and Arrivals Guide

Malaga VIP Transfers • Airport Guides • 5 min read

Navigating a busy airport can be stressful, but being prepared makes all the difference. As the primary gateway to Andalusia and the Costa del Sol, we've put together everything you need to know about departures from Malaga Airport, flight arrivals, and finding your way around.

Malaga Airport Code (AGP) and Overview

Commonly asked: How many airports in Malaga? There is only one main international airport serving the region. The official Malaga Airport code is AGP (Aeropuerto de Málaga-Costa del Sol). It is the fourth busiest airport in Spain, handling millions of passengers travelling to destinations like Marbella, Torremolinos, and Estepona.

Malaga Airport Departures Today

If you are checking Malaga flight departures, it is always advised to arrive at least 2.5 hours before your scheduled flight time, especially during the summer. For malaga airport flights departures, most airlines operate out of Terminal 3, which handles the majority of international traffic. Terminal 2 is also used by certain low-cost carriers.

Question: Malaga airport which terminal? Your booking confirmation will state your terminal, but generally, easyJet and Ryanair often utilize T2 for check-in, though security and boarding usually route through the main T3 hub.

Malaga Airport Arrivals Today

Tracking Malaga airport arrivals today is easy via the official AENA app or website. All malaga airport flights arrivals funnel into the main arrivals hall in Terminal 3.

Meeting Your Driver

When you book a transfer with Malaga VIP Transfers, your driver will be waiting for you inside the arrivals hall, immediately after you exit baggage reclaim. They will be holding a tablet or sign displaying your lead passenger name. Because we monitor malaga airport flights arrivals in real-time, your driver will be there even if your flight is delayed.

Malaga Airport Restaurants & Amenities

While waiting for departures from Malaga airport, you have access to a variety of Malaga airport restaurants and shops. Post-security in Terminal 3, you'll find everything from VIP lounges and duty-free shopping to tapas bars, cafes, and international fast food.
