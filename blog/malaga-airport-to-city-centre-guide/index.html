Malaga Airport to City Centre — All Options, Prices & Journey Times

Malaga VIP Transfers•Route Guide•8 min read

Getting from Malaga Airport to Malaga city centre is quick and inexpensive — the airport is just 8 km from the centre. You have four main options: the Cercanías train, the Express Bus A, a metered taxi, or a pre-booked private transfer. This guide breaks down each option so you can make the right choice for your arrival.

Distance: 8 km  |  Fastest option: Train (11 min)  |  Cheapest option: Train (€2.70)  |  Best for families/luggage: Private transfer (from €66)

How far is Malaga Airport from city centre?

Malaga Airport to Malaga city centre is approximately 8 km by road, 7 km by train. The airport is located south-west of the city, directly on the coast. In good traffic, the drive takes 15–20 minutes. By train it is just 11 minutes. This makes Malaga Airport one of the most conveniently located airports in Europe relative to its city centre.

Option 1: Train — Malaga Airport train to city centre

The Malaga airport train to city centre — the C1 Cercanías suburban train — is the fastest and cheapest way to get into the city. The station is inside Terminal 3, directly accessible from arrivals (follow signs for "Tren/Train").

Option 2: Bus — Express Bus A

Express Bus A connects Malaga Airport to the city centre (Alameda Principal / Paseo del Parque) in approximately 20 minutes. The bus stop is outside Terminal 3. Cost: approximately €3. The bus runs frequently during the day but less so at night — check the timetable if arriving late.

The bus is slightly less convenient than the train with heavy luggage but is a reasonable option for arrivals during the day. For Malaga airport to Malaga city centre journeys with light luggage, train or bus are both fine options.

Option 3: Metered taxi — taxi Malaga airport to city centre

The metered taxi Malaga airport to city centre costs approximately €18–25 depending on traffic, time of day, and whether any supplements apply (airport supplement typically €3–5). The taxi rank is immediately outside Terminal 3 arrivals. Taxis can carry up to 4 passengers.

For a single person, the metered taxi is significantly more expensive than the train (€20 vs €2.70). For two or more people, the cost-per-person gap narrows considerably. Taxis do not require advance booking but can queue at peak times.

Option 4: Private transfer — Malaga airport to Malaga central

A pre-booked private transfer from Malaga Airport to Malaga city centre with Malaga VIP Transfers starts from €66 per vehicle. Unlike a taxi, the price is fixed and confirmed before you travel — no meter running. Your driver meets you in arrivals with a name board, helps with your luggage, and takes you directly to your hotel, apartment or cruise terminal in the city.

For the Malaga airport to Malaga central route, the private transfer makes particular sense for:

  • Families with children and multiple large cases
  • Late-night arrivals (after the last train at ~23:30)
  • Guests staying at specific addresses not near a train station
  • Groups of 2 or more (cost per head becomes competitive with taxi)
  • Business travellers who want a guaranteed car and quality vehicle
  • Malaga airport to Malaga port (cruise terminal) — direct, with luggage assistance

Things to do in Malaga city centre

If you're heading to Malaga city centre for a night or a few days before heading to your resort, the city has much to offer: the Picasso Museum (Pablo Ruiz Picasso was born in Malaga), Malaga Cathedral, Alcazaba fortress, the historic Soho neighbourhood, the Atarazanas market, and the Muelle Uno waterfront. The city is very walkable from most central hotels.
