Day Trip from Malaga to Granada: The Complete Guide

Malaga VIP Transfers•Day Trip•8 min read

A day trip from Malaga to Granada is one of the most rewarding excursions available from the Costa del Sol. Granada is home to the Alhambra — arguably Spain's most magnificent monument — as well as the atmospheric Albaicín quarter, free tapas culture, and the haunting sounds of flamenco. Whether you are staying in Malaga city or arriving via Malaga Airport, this guide explains exactly how to make the most of your Malaga to Granada day trip.

How Far is Malaga to Granada?

The distance from Malaga to Granada is approximately 125 kilometres, following the A-92 motorway across the Vega plain. The drive time is typically 1 hour 20 minutes to 1 hour 45 minutes under normal conditions. The journey passes through beautiful Andalusian countryside — olive groves, rolling hills, and the occasional glimpse of snow-capped Sierra Nevada peaks in winter.

If you are departing from Malaga Airport rather than central Malaga, add approximately 20 minutes to the journey from the airport to Granada city centre — roughly 145 km total.

Best Ways to Get from Malaga to Granada

Option 1 — Private Transfer from Malaga to Granada

A private driver is the most flexible, comfortable, and time-efficient way to do a Malaga to Granada day trip. With Malaga VIP Transfers, you can arrange a return day trip with a professional chauffeur in a premium Mercedes vehicle. Your driver can wait in Granada while you explore, or drop you off in the morning and collect you at a pre-agreed time in the evening. This gives you complete freedom over your itinerary — essential for Alhambra visits where timeslots are often spread across the day.

  • Ideal for families, couples, or business travellers

Option 2 — Train (Renfe)

Renfe operates regular services between Malaga María Zambrano station and Granada. Journey times vary from approximately 1 hour 30 minutes to 1 hour 55 minutes depending on the service. Trains run roughly every 2–3 hours. Note that Malaga Airport to Granada by train requires first taking the Cercanías C1 to María Zambrano station (12 min), then connecting with the Granada service — total journey from airport: 2–2.5 hours.

Train tickets cost approximately €15–30 each way. Booking in advance through the Renfe website or app is recommended during peak season.

Option 3 — Bus (Alsa)

Alsa operates direct coach services from Malaga bus station to Granada, with journey times of approximately 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours. Tickets cost around €10–18 one way. This is the budget option, though the less frequent schedule and lack of flexibility make it less suitable for day trips where you want maximum time in Granada.

What to See in Granada in One Day

A well-planned day in Granada allows you to experience the city's key highlights:

  • The Alhambra Palace: Spain's most visited monument — book tickets weeks in advance. Allow 3–4 hours minimum. The Nasrid Palaces close their ticket windows in specific timeslots; arriving late means missing the most spectacular rooms.
  • Generalife Gardens: Adjacent to the Alhambra, these Islamic gardens with their fountains and water channels are included in the standard Alhambra ticket.
  • Albaicín Quarter: Granada's ancient Moorish neighbourhood — a UNESCO World Heritage Site of white-washed houses and cobbled alleys. The Mirador de San Nicolás offers the most famous view of the Alhambra.
  • Granada Cathedral: One of Spain's finest Renaissance cathedrals, located in the city centre. Admission is inexpensive and queues are manageable.
  • Free Tapas: Granada is one of the last Spanish cities to offer a free tapa with every drink. Order a caña (small beer) at any traditional bar and a plate of food arrives automatically.

Important: The Alhambra sells only approximately 6,600 tickets per day and they sell out weeks or months ahead in summer. If you are planning a day trip from Malaga to Granada, book Alhambra tickets the moment your travel dates are confirmed at alhambra-patronato.es.
