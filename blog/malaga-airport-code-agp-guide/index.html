Malaga Airport Code AGP — Complete Airport Guide

Malaga VIP Transfers•Airport Guide•8 min read

Travelling through Malaga Airport? Everything you need to know — from what malaga airport code AGP stands for, to which terminal handles your flight, arrivals and departures information, and how to get to your hotel on the Costa del Sol.

Malaga Airport at a glance:
IATA Code: AGP  |  ICAO Code: LEMG  |  Full name: Aeropuerto de Málaga-Costa del Sol (Pablo Ruiz Picasso Airport)  |  Location: 8 km south-west of Malaga city centre  |  Annual passengers: ~22 million

What is Malaga airport code AGP?

Malaga airport code AGP is the IATA (International Air Transport Association) three-letter designator for Aeropuerto de Málaga-Costa del Sol. You will see AGP on your boarding pass, flight booking confirmation, and baggage tags when flying to or from Malaga. The ICAO code (used by air traffic control) is LEMG — LE for Spain's airspace, MG for Málaga.

The AGP airport code doesn't come from a place name at all — it comes down to IATA's assignment rules. By the time Malaga needed a code, MAL, MLG and MGA had all already been assigned to other airports, so IATA gave Malaga A and G (from the city's name) plus an available third letter, P, producing AGP. A popular myth connects the code to Pablo Picasso, but that connection post-dates AGP by decades: the airport was officially renamed Malaga airport Pablo Ruiz Picasso in honour of the renowned artist, born in Málaga in 1881, long after the code was already in use.

Malaga Airport Terminal 3 — layout and facilities

Malaga Airport Terminal 3 is the sole terminal serving all commercial flights into and out of AGP. It is a large, modern building with international arrivals on the ground floor and departures on the upper level. Key facilities include:

  • Arrivals (Ground floor): Baggage reclaim, customs, car hire desks, taxi rank, train station access, meeting point for private transfers
  • Departures (Upper level): Check-in desks, security screening, gates, duty-free shopping, bars and restaurants
  • Shopping: Major brands in the departures lounge including Zara, El Corte Inglés, WHSmith, pharmacies
  • Dining: Multiple cafes, bars and restaurants in both arrivals and departures
  • Train station: Cercanías C1 train inside Terminal 3, direct to Malaga city and beyond
  • Car hire: All major providers (Hertz, Avis, Enterprise, Europcar, Sixt and others) with desks inside arrivals

Malaga Airport arrivals

Malaga Airport arrivals are processed on the ground floor of Terminal 3. International arrivals (non-Schengen) go through passport control before baggage reclaim. EU/EEA passport holders use electronic gates; non-EU passengers queue at staffed desks. Once through customs, you exit into the main arrivals hall where our drivers wait with name boards for private transfer clients.

To check live Malaga Airport arrivals, AENA's official website (aena.es) and FlightRadar24 both show real-time flight statuses. We monitor all flights automatically for our private transfer bookings.

Malaga Airport departures

Malaga Airport departures are on the upper level of Terminal 3. AENA recommends arriving at least 2 hours before departure for European flights, and 3 hours for intercontinental. For live Malaga airport departures today, the AENA app and the airport's website show gate information in real time. Our private transfer service monitors departure flights and advises the correct pick-up time from your accommodation to ensure you reach Terminal 3 in good time.

Airlines serving Malaga AGP

Malaga Airport (AGP) is served by virtually every major European carrier, including: Ryanair (the largest operator at AGP), easyJet, Jet2, TUI Airways, Vueling, Iberia, British Airways, Lufthansa, Air France, KLM, Norwegian, Wizz Air, Eurowings, and many more. Seasonal charters from Scandinavia, Germany, Netherlands, Poland, and the UK operate throughout the summer months (April–October).
